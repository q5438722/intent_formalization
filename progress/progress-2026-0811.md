## Questions for discussion

1. **How should we review generated specifications?**  
   We have already generated specs for modules such as `slice`. Is it
   sufficient that a generated spec can be proved, or do we also need a
   separate review of whether it captures the intended semantics and is useful
   to downstream callers?

2. **Which modules are worthwhile targets for spec generation?**  
   Do we have criteria for selecting the next modules? My preliminary view is
   that the most valuable targets may be APIs involving external system state,
