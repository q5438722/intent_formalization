# Weekly Progress: Rust Standard-Library Specification Generation and Paper Revision

Snapshot: 2026-09-22 10:06 UTC

## 1. Rust standard-library specification generation

Based on Jay's comments, we updated the spec generation and review workflow:

- **Disallow uninterpreted specifications.** Generated specifications must not
  introduce uninterpreted helpers as new trust boundaries. For mutable
  references and equality, the pipeline follows existing vstd modeling
  patterns and reuses established view and equality functions.
- **Move from module-level to sub-module-level generation.** We first divide
  each module into groups of related APIs, such as `Vec::drain` and
  `Drain::as_slice`. Specifications for all APIs in each group are then
  generated and reviewed together.
- **Add an explicit correctness review against the implementation.** After
  each round of generation or repair, the specification must be verified
  against the implementation and rerun through completeness checking.

We are generating new versions of the Slice and Vec specifications:

- [Slice specifications](https://github.com/q5438722/rust-std-spec/tree/main/experiments/rust-std-slice-specgen-astra-2026-09-22/specs/slice)
- [Vec specifications](https://github.com/q5438722/rust-std-spec/tree/main/experiments/rust-std-vec-specgen-astra-2026-09-22/specs)

- **Generation speed.** With the updated workflow and its additional review
  steps, the current throughput is approximately 11 API specifications per day.

## 2. Paper revision [for verusyn's submission]

- **Semantic deduplication.** Our experiments show that semantic
  deduplication does not improve end-to-end effectiveness and leads to a
  significant performance drop on VerusBench. We added these results and the
  corresponding analysis to Appendix D.3.
- **Correctness and completeness evaluation.** On a 10% subset of the Part 1
  dataset, the generated specifications achieve 98.93% correctness (5 positive test cases) and 80.42% completeness (5 negative test cases). Evaluation on 
  the full Part 1 dataset is in progress and expected to finish by 9.24.
